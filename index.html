CAA Virtual · BUAP · English A1–C2

Practice English at your own pace

Choose a unit, follow the five steps, and check what you can do at the end. Every unit takes about 50–60 minutes and you can come back any time.

How every unit works

  1. PresentationMeet the topic in a real situation.
  2. ObjectivesSee what you will be able to do.
  3. ExplanationVocabulary, grammar and examples.
  4. PracticeFour skills:ListeningReadingWritingSpeaking
  5. Self-assessmentRate six "I can…" statements from 1 to 5.
Writing evidence: write and submit your text directly in the unit.
Speaking evidence: practice with the computer, then record your task and upload it to your course platform.

Before you start · Antes de empezar

🌐 Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

The audio and the speaking practice work best in these browsers, on a computer or an Android phone.

Abre el sitio en Chrome o Edge para escuchar bien los audios y practicar con el micrófono. Otros navegadores (Safari, Firefox) pueden no funcionar bien.

🎤 Allow the microphone

When the browser asks, click Allow. If you clicked Block by mistake, click the 🔒 next to the web address and allow the microphone.

Cuando el navegador lo pida, da clic en Permitir. Si lo bloqueaste, da clic en el candado 🔒 junto a la dirección y permite el micrófono.

🎧 Use headphones in a quiet place

You will hear the conversations more clearly and the computer will understand you better.

Usa audífonos y busca un lugar tranquilo.
